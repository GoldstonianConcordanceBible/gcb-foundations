# Versioning Policy
## SydTek Seminary + GCB Foundations

Modules and institutional documents may receive periodic updates.

Recommended Version Format:

Module IX v1.0  
Updated May 2026

---

## Versioning Purposes

Version tracking supports:
- curriculum consistency,
- academic integrity,
- transcript alignment,
- historical documentation,
- and institutional transparency.

---

## Recommended Version Categories

| Version Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Major Revision | Structural or competency changes |
| Minor Revision | Assignment or quiz updates |
| Patch Revision | Typographical or formatting corrections |

---

## Example

Module IX v1.1  
Updated Quiz Bank and Reflection Instructions

Module IX v2.0  
Expanded Competency Framework and Transcript Integration
