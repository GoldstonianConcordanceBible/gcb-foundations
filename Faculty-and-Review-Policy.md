# Faculty and Review Policy
## SydTek Seminary Foundations Program

Assessment and verification activities may be reviewed by:
- instructional staff,
- authorized SydTek evaluators,
- theological reviewers,
- or approved competency reviewers.

Review processes may include:
- quiz verification,
- reflection review,
- competency validation,
- participation review,
- and transcript confirmation.

The SydTek Seminary ecosystem may incorporate:
- human review,
- AI-assisted review,
- or hybrid competency verification systems.

Final credential approval remains subject to institutional verification standards.
