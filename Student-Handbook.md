# Student Handbook
## SydTek Seminary Foundations Program

### Academic Integrity

Students should complete all assignments honestly and respectfully. Plagiarism, impersonation, falsification of records, or misuse of verification systems may result in removal from credential pathways.

---

### Participation Expectations

Students are expected to:
- complete required lectures,
- complete assignments,
- engage respectfully in discussions,
- and demonstrate competency through reflection and assessment activities.

---

### Grading and Verification

Recommended Passing Threshold:
80%

Verification includes:
- quizzes,
- written reflections,
- participation,
- and final synthesis assignments.

---

### Transcript-Style Records

Students completing requirements may receive:
- transcript-style completion records,
- digital certificates,
- stackable theological microcredentials,
- and optional Solana credential verification.

---

### Public Participation Layer

Some participation activities may occur publicly through YouTube comments or public discussion systems.

Students should never publicly post:
- passwords,
- private wallet keys,
- legal identification,
- or sensitive personal information.

---

### Privacy and Verification

Verification records, grading operations, transcript systems, and registrar workflows are maintained within protected SydTek institutional systems.

---

### Appeals Process

Students may request review of grading or completion status through SydTek Seminary support channels.

---

### Code of Conduct

Students should engage respectfully with:
- Scripture,
- instructors,
- fellow learners,
- and discussion participants.

Harassment, abuse, or malicious disruption may result in removal from learning pathways.
