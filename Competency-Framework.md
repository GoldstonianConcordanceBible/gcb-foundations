# SydTek Seminary Competency Framework

## Core Competencies

### Biblical Literacy
Students should demonstrate familiarity with Scripture, canonical themes, and biblical interpretation.

### Theological Reasoning
Students should explain and synthesize theological doctrines using Scripture and structured theological language.

### Canonical Analysis
Students should analyze the relationship between biblical books, canon traditions, and theological continuity.

### Spiritual Formation
Students should demonstrate understanding of repentance, sanctification, discipleship, and transformation.

### Reflection and Synthesis
Students should integrate Scripture, theology, and reflection into written and discussion-based responses.

### Discipleship Application
Students should connect doctrine to spiritual practice, obedience, and Christian living.

### Mirror → Water → Fire Integration
Students should apply the GCB framework of:
- Mirror
- Water
- Fire

to theology, salvation, formation, and discipleship.
