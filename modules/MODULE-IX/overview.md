# MODULE IX
## The Doctrine of Salvation

### Grace, Faith, Repentance, Sanctification, and Eternal Life

Microcredential ID:
MICRO-GCB-009

Credential Type:
Stackable Theological Microcredential

Estimated Verified Learning Hours:
20–24 Hours

Estimated Direct Instruction Runtime:
3 Hours 43 Minutes

This module explores:
- salvation,
- grace,
- repentance,
- surrender,
- sanctification,
- obedience,
- perseverance,
- eternal life,
- and spiritual refinement.

The module follows the Goldstonian Concordance Bible framework:

Mirror → Water → Fire

Mirror:
The Word reveals sin and spiritual need.

Water:
Christ cleanses, restores, and renews.

Fire:
The Spirit sanctifies and refines believers through obedience and endurance.
