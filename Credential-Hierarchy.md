## Credential Features

Credentials may include:
- transcript-style records,
- verified learning hours (VLH),
- digital certificates,
- stackable completion pathways,
- and optional Solana verification.

Knowledge remains publicly accessible.

Verification and credentialing function as the formal academic layer.
