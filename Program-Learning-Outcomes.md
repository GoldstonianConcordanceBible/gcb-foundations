# Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)
## SydTek Seminary Foundations Pathway

Graduates of the SydTek Seminary Foundations Pathway should be able to:

1. Explain foundational Christian theological doctrines using biblical language and theological reasoning.

2. Analyze Scripture through canonical, theological, and concordance-based frameworks.

3. Demonstrate understanding of the 81-Book Ethiopian Canon and related theological traditions.

4. Apply the Goldstonian Concordance Bible framework of Mirror → Water → Fire to spiritual formation and discipleship.

5. Demonstrate competency through reflection, written synthesis, discussion participation, and assessment activities.

6. Connect doctrine to Christian practice, obedience, sanctification, and discipleship.

7. Demonstrate theological literacy through Scripture engagement and structured theological analysis.

8. Participate in competency-based theological learning environments using transcript-style completion systems and optional blockchain credential verification.
